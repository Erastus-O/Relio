each category its own way.

Every category has its own intake, evidence and credentials. Nobody gets asked for a bedroom count to book a maths tutor.

Cleaning · live

Bedrooms, bathrooms and condition set a fixed price.

Recurring plans, room checklists and before-and-after photos, or a supervisor sign-off where photos aren’t allowed.

Home repairs · pilot

Describe the issue, get an assessment or quote, approve before extra work.

Parts and time are recorded, with a completion photo or customer sign-off.

Tutoring · pilot

At a venue or online, booked by the session.

Attendance and sign-off instead of photos. Online sessions sit in their own section, not ranked by distance.

Gardening, pet care and more: businesses can register interest now and draft listings. A category opens for booking only after its template and checks are reviewed.
